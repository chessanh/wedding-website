Transportation & Lodging

The Redmond Municipal Airport (RDM) is recommended and is ~25 minutes away. Portland, Oregon is ~3 hours away. Uber and Lyft are available to/from RDM, but we'd recommend scheduling ahead of time if you have very early or late flights. If at any point you're unable to get an Uber/Lyft, please call us at 207-712-7722 and we'll send someone to help!

The Hotel Peppertree is recommended and within walking distance to most events (most of the wedding party, including us, will be there). Alternatively, there are several hotels in the Old Mill district and downtown, as well as Airbnb/VRBO rentals scattered around Bend.

Within Bend, Uber/Lyft are readily available. If you'd like to explore the larger Bend area, we'd recommend renting a car.


Transportation for events:

For Friday's Wine Night, we recommend Uber/Lyft, as it's a 35min walk from the hotel and there is limited street parking.

For Saturday, we will provide shuttles from the hotel starting at 3pm (or it is a 16min walk) and to the after party/hotel afterwards (or it is a 14min walk). If you aren't staying at the hotel we recommend Uber/Lyft or there is abundant parking.

For Sunday, brunch is an 8min walk from the hotel. If you aren't staying at the hotel we recommend Uber/Lyft or there is abundant parking.
